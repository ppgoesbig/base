Check my photo

1. The background isn't pure white

The most common cause by far. Amazon wants RGB 255, 255, 255. Studio backdrops usually photograph at 230–250: white to you, grey to a pixel check. Fix: lift the backdrop to exact #FFFFFF. See how to make a background pure white.

2. Too much empty space

The product should fill at least 85% of the frame. Products shot from a distance, or padded onto a big square canvas, end up small. Fix: crop to the product and scale it so its longest side spans 85–90% of the image.

3. The image is too small

Images need at least 500 px on the longest side, and 1,000 px or more to enable zoom. Thumbnails exported from a website or phone screenshots often fall short. Fix: export from the original at 2,000 px or more on the longest side.

4. Text, logos, badges or watermarks

"Best seller" badges, sale stickers, brand logos added in editing, borders and watermarks aren't allowed on the main image. A logo printed on the product itself is fine. Fix: remove the overlays and save them for secondary images.

5. Props and extras that aren't included

Only the item being sold should appear. A styled table, a plant next to the vase, or accessories that don't ship with the product can get the image flagged. Fix: reshoot or retouch down to the product alone.

6. Multiple views or a collage

Several angles stitched together, or a product shown in multiple colours, belong in the other image slots. Fix: one view of one product for the main image.

7. The product is cropped

The whole product should be in the frame. If it touches the edges it can look cut off. Fix: leave a small white margin on every side.

8. Category-specific rules

Some categories add their own rules on top. Adult clothing is shown on a model or laid flat, not on a mannequin. Shoes are shown as a single shoe facing left at about 45 degrees. Check your category's style guide in Seller Central.
