      Questions

      Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

      No. Photos are read, checked and fixed on your own computer and are never sent anywhere. On this site, checks and the backdrop cleanup keep working if you go offline after the page loads. The AI cut-out downloads its model (about 19 MB) the first time a photo needs it, then works offline for the rest of your visit. Pro includes the model and works fully offline. If anonymous visit counting is turned on for this site, it never includes photo data.

      What does Amazon require for the main image?

      A pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), the product filling at least 85% of the frame, at least 1,000 px on the longest side for zoom, and nothing but the product: no text, logos, watermarks, borders or extra props. See the full Amazon guide.

      My background looks white. Why does it fail?

      Most "white" studio backdrops photograph as light grey, around 235–250. They look white on screen, but automated checks look for exact #FFFFFF. PureFrame shows the share of background pixels that are exactly white and fixes it with a white-point correction plus a clean flood fill of the backdrop. More in how to make a background pure white.

      Will it cut a product out of a busy or lifestyle photo?

      Yes. When the background isn't a plain backdrop, PureFrame cuts the product out with a compact AI model (U-2-Netp) that runs on your device. It works well on clear subjects. Zoom in to check fine details such as straps, fur or glass, and crop out other objects first. Studio shots keep the backdrop cleanup, which preserves real shadows.

      What does "re-checked after compression" mean?

      Saving a JPEG can turn pixels next to the product slightly off-white (249–254), even when the image was pure white before saving. Automated checks read the saved file, so PureFrame decodes what it saved and measures every background pixel more than 8 px from the product. Wherever a marketplace requires or recommends a white background, it raises the quality, or saves a PNG where the size limit allows, to keep every one of those pixels exactly #FFFFFF. If no version fits the limit, it saves the cleanest one that does and shows how many pixels are off-white. Etsy has no background rule, so there PureFrame reports the numbers without changing the file.

      Can I check photos made in other apps?

      Yes. Drop in exports from any editor, background remover or retoucher. Checking is free and unlimited, and each photo is graded against all 7 marketplaces at once.

      Does it work with white products?

      Yes. Only backdrop pixels connected to the edge of the photo are whitened, so white areas inside the product keep their shading. If a white product's edge blends into the backdrop, lower the "Background cleanup" setting.

      Does passing mean my listing will be approved?

      PureFrame checks the measurable rules. Marketplaces also have content rules (no text, logos or props that aren't included) and category style guides that need a human eye, and they update their rules from time to time. Each requirement page links to the official source.
